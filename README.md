# Harry Potter
 
## Overview
 
*Harry Potter* is a fantasy series about a boy who discovers he is a wizard on his eleventh birthday and attends Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. The series was written by J.K. Rowling, and the first film, *Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone*, was released in 2001 and directed by Chris Columbus.
 
## Why I Like It
 
I enjoy this series because **the world feels magical and detailed** and *the characters grow up with the reader*. Every book adds a new layer to the story, and the small details planted early often matter later.
 
* It does not do well to dwell on dreams and forget to live." - Albus Dumbledore, *Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets*
 
## Key Details
 
- Genre: Fantasy, adventure, coming-of-age
- Creator: J.K. Rowling
- Main cast: Daniel Radcliffe, Rupert Grint, Emma Watson
- Number of books: 7
 
## Favorite Characters
 
1. Harry Potter, the boy who lived and the story's main hero
2. Hermione Granger, the brilliant and loyal friend who always has a plan
3. Severus Snape, the complicated professor whose true loyalties are revealed slowly
